About Creovum

Good software starts
with a clear idea.

Creovum Digital Studio is an independent creative home for small apps, useful tools and the occasional experiment.

The studio

Independent by design.

Creovum grew from a simple interest in making things work better. Sometimes that means an Android app you use every day. Other times it's a tool that makes a home server less complicated.

There's no big team behind the name. The focus is on making considered software, learning from real use and improving it over time.

Each project can look and feel different. Creovum is the common thread behind them.
