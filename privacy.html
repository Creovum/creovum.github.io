Site information

Website privacy.

How the Creovum website handles information.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not use Creovum-operated analytics, advertising trackers or cookie-based personalization. Creovum does not set tracking cookies on these pages.

External services

The site uses static hosting on GitHub Pages and loads the Manrope typeface from Google Fonts when you are online. Those providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses, needed to deliver pages and fonts. Links to other services, including Google Play, lead to sites with their own privacy practices.

Contact

If you email Creovum, your message and email address are handled by your email provider and Creovum's email service so that your enquiry can be answered. The website has no contact form and does not collect messages directly.

App policies

This page covers the website only. For information about the apps, read the NITE Privacy Policy or Mercode Privacy Policy.

Questions

Contact hello@creovum.dev for website privacy questions.
