MERCODE · Service Codes, Decoded.

Privacy Policy.

How Mercode handles information on your Android device.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

1. Who we are

Mercode is an independent Android reference app developed by Creovum. It helps users understand reported Mercedes-Benz service codes. Mercode is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mercedes-Benz. This policy describes how Mercode handles information.

2. Information Mercode uses

Mercode is designed to work locally on your device. To provide service-code lookups, it uses:

  • Service-code searches: the code you enter, such as B2.
  • Engine-type selection: the filter you choose, such as diesel or petrol.
  • On-screen state: whether you have opened the code directory or particular information panels.

These are processed in the app to display relevant reference information. Mercode does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address or vehicle identification number (VIN). The current app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or photos.

3. How service-code searches work

Mercode matches your input against a reference database included with the app. The lookups run on your device and do not require an internet connection. The current Android app does not request the INTERNET permission and does not send your searches or engine-type selections to a Creovum server.

4. Collection, sharing and analytics

Mercode does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or an app-operated online account service. Creovum does not receive your service-code searches or in-app filter selections through Mercode, and the app does not transmit them to advertisers or other third parties.

If you choose to contact Creovum by email, we receive the information you send, including your email address, and use it to respond to your inquiry. This is separate from your use of Mercode's offline lookup feature.

5. App stores and this website

If you download Mercode through Google Play or another app store, the store operator may process information relating to downloads, installations, device compatibility or diagnostics under its own privacy policy. Mercode does not offer in-app purchases. For Google Play, see Google's Privacy Policy.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical website data under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This is separate from Mercode's offline service-code lookups. Creovum has not added advertising or analytics scripts to this page.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

Mercode's service-code reference data is bundled with the app. Search text, filter choices and open panels are held as on-device screen state while you use Mercode. Android may temporarily preserve some screen state to restore the interface after a configuration change or process restart. The app does not maintain a Creovum-hosted search history or an online user profile.

You can clear Mercode's app data in Android settings or uninstall it to remove app-managed local state. If you contact us by email, we retain the correspondence only as needed to handle your inquiry or meet applicable obligations. You can ask about that correspondence or request its deletion using the contact address below.

7. Security

Mercode's current lookup feature runs on the device without transferring search information to an online service. This limits the information exposed during ordinary use. External services such as Google Play, GitHub Pages and your email provider manage their own data and security practices.

8. Changes to this policy

If Mercode's features or data practices change, this policy will be reviewed and updated. A revised effective date will be shown on this page. The statements above describe the reviewed version of Mercode, not features that may be introduced later.
