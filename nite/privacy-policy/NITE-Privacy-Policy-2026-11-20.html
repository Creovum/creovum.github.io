Privacy Policy.

How NITE handles information on your Android device.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who we are

NITE is an Android bedside-clock and screen saver app developed and published by Creovum. This policy explains what information NITE uses, when information may be shared with other services, and how you can contact us about privacy.

2. Information NITE uses on your device

NITE's clock and customization features work primarily on your device. Depending on the options you enable, the app uses:

  • Clock preferences: time format, fonts, colors, layout, element sizes, brightness, night schedule and related settings.
  • Device information: local time, date, language, screen orientation, charging status and battery level.
  • Next alarm: the upcoming alarm reported by Android, if you enable alarm display.
  • Chosen photos: images you select to use as a clock background.

NITE uses these details to show and personalize your clock. Creovum does not operate a server that receives your clock settings, battery readings, alarm details or selected backgrounds.

3. Your background photos

When you choose a personal background, NITE opens Android's system photo picker. You choose which image to share with NITE; the app does not request access to your entire photo library.

NITE makes a local copy of the selected image in its private app storage so it can display your chosen background. It does not upload that image to a Creovum server. The original photo remains in your own gallery unless you delete it yourself.

4. Feedback and support emails

Under About NITE > Feedback & Support, you can choose Report a problem or Suggest a feature. These options open your email app with a draft message. NITE does not automatically send a report or an email.

A bug-report draft includes NITE's version, the device manufacturer and model, and the Android version. You can review and edit this information before choosing whether to send the email. You may also add details, screenshots or other information yourself.

If you send an email, Creovum receives your email address, the message you write, and any details you leave in the draft or attach. We use this information to respond, investigate problems and consider improvements to NITE. Where applicable, we rely on our legitimate interest in handling support requests and improving the app. Email transmission and storage also involve your email provider and Creovum's email provider, which process messages under their own terms and privacy practices.

Sending feedback is optional. NITE does not require an email address or account to use its clock features.

5. Google Play app updates

NITE can check Google Play for a newer app version and offer an optional in-app update. This uses Google's Play In-App Updates service; NITE does not send update-check information to a Creovum server.

According to Google, its update service uses technical details such as device metadata, the installed app version, and information about installed app modules or asset packs to determine whether an update is available and its expected size. Google Play handles this information under its own terms and privacy practices. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Accounts, analytics and data sharing

NITE does not require a Creovum account and does not include in-app advertising or a Creovum-operated analytics service in the current version. Its clock preferences and selected backgrounds are not uploaded to Creovum.

The main exceptions to fully local use are optional support emails that you choose to send, and Google Play services used for app installation, updates and related platform functions. Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may also process technical or service information independently under their own privacy policies.

7. Purchases and advertising

NITE does not currently offer in-app purchases, subscriptions or in-app advertisements. If these features are introduced later, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data handling.

8. Storage, retention and deletion

NITE stores its local settings and app-managed background images on your device. You can remove this information by clearing NITE's app data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to Android's backup and restore features. Uninstalling NITE does not delete original photos from your gallery.

If you email us for support or feedback, we keep the correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, investigate the issue, manage support history or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete personal information you have provided in a support email by writing to support@creovum.dev. We will review and handle requests subject to applicable legal requirements.

NITE does not offer user accounts, so there is no in-app account to delete.

9. Security

NITE stores app-managed clock data within Android's app storage and uses Android's system photo picker to limit access to chosen images. We aim to restrict access to support messages to what is needed to handle your inquiry. Your email provider and ours are responsible for their respective email services and security measures.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You can turn optional clock information on or off, remove selected backgrounds, and clear locally stored settings using your device's controls. You can also choose not to send any feedback email.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have sent to Creovum, as well as to object to or request restriction of certain processing. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For questions or requests, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when NITE's features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
