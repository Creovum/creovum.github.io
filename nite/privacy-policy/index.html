Privacy Policy.

How NITE handles information on your Android device.

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Who we are

NITE is an Android bedside-clock app developed and published by Creovum. This policy explains how the app handles information and what choices are available to you.

2. Information NITE uses

NITE is designed to work primarily on your device. Depending on the features you enable, NITE may use the following information to display and customize your clock:

  • Clock preferences: display format, typeface, colors, size, brightness, night schedule and related settings.
  • Device information: local time, date, language, orientation, charging state and battery level.
  • Alarm information: the next alarm reported by Android, if you turn on alarm display.
  • Background images: pictures you explicitly choose to show behind the clock.

This information is used to provide NITE's clock and customization features.

3. Your background photos

If you choose to use a personal background image, NITE uses Android's system photo-selection experience to let you pick a photo. NITE is intended to access only the photos you select, rather than your entire photo library. Selected photos are used to display the background on your device and are not uploaded to a Creovum server.

4. Collection, sharing and analytics

NITE does not require a Creovum user account. Its clock and customization features are designed to work locally on your device. Creovum does not operate a backend service for receiving your clock settings, personal backgrounds, next-alarm details or battery readings. NITE does not display in-app advertising. If third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain what information they process.

Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may independently process technical or service information under their own privacy policies. For optional purchases, see the section below.

5. Purchases

If optional paid features are offered, purchases are handled by Google Play. Google handles your payment details. NITE may receive purchase status or related transaction identifiers needed to unlock or restore paid features, but Creovum does not receive your full payment-card details through the app. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's data practices.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

NITE's local settings and app-managed background data are stored on your device for as long as you keep them there. You can clear NITE's app data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data, subject to Android's backup and restore behavior. Photos in your own gallery are not deleted when you uninstall NITE.

NITE does not provide user accounts. If you have a privacy question or deletion request, contact Creovum using the details below.

7. Security

NITE is intended to process clock settings and chosen media on the device. Creovum aims to limit data access to what is needed for the app's functions. Google Play and Android provide additional controls for app installation, access to media, and purchases.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if NITE's features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted here, with a revised effective date.
