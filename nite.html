The idea

Make nights simpler.

NITE turns an Android device into a customizable bedside clock and supports Android screensaver mode on compatible devices. Choose the clock layout, font, color and background to make the display your own.

Options include 12- or 24-hour time, date, alarm information, battery display and burn-in protection. NITE is in closed testing, and its features are still being refined.
