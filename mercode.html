The idea

Less guesswork around service codes.

Mercedes A/B service codes can be difficult to understand. Mercode is being developed to explain what the codes mean and which maintenance items they may relate to, in language intended for everyday owners.

The interface is designed around selecting a diesel or petrol engine and searching for a code. The code database and explanations are still being reviewed, so the app is not presented here as a finished service guide.
